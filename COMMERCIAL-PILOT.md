# N’KEMBA Independent AI Audit — Commercial Pilot

## What is offered

N’KEMBA provides independent, evidence-bounded audits of AI runtimes, agent controls, governance mechanisms and evidence chains.

The service is built around a simple distinction:

- **Documented capability** — what the vendor documentation says.
- **Runtime behaviour** — what a controlled reproducible test demonstrates.
- **Evidence quality** — what can actually be reconstructed and proved afterwards.

The public NVIDIA OpenShell series is the first demonstration of this method.

## Pilot engagement

A pilot audit can be scoped around one concrete claim, control, workflow or evidence boundary.

Typical deliverables:

1. exact claim and scope;
2. source and version freeze;
3. reproducible test design;
4. controlled execution where access is available;
5. raw evidence inventory;
6. result classification;
7. proof gaps and limitations;
8. reproducibility package;
9. public or private report, as agreed.

## Important boundary

N’KEMBA does not sell vendor certification, penetration testing without authorization, or claims based only on speculation.

A finding may be:

- **CONFIRMED**;
- **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**;
- **NOT PROVED**;
- **EXPERIMENT PENDING**.

The classification follows the evidence.

## Why this matters

AI systems increasingly combine models, agents, tools, credentials, policies and external services. A statement that a control exists is not the same thing as proving that a particular action was governed by that control and that the resulting evidence is complete and attributable.

N’KEMBA audits that boundary.
